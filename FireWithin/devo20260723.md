Today’s Scripture reminds us that God’s law was never meant to save
us, but to reveal His holy character and our need for His grace.

Paul explains that believers are no longer under the law as a means of
being justified before God. Through faith in Jesus Christ, we have
been set free from the law’s condemnation. Yet this freedom does not
diminish the value of God’s law. Instead, it helps us understand its
true purpose.

God’s law exposes what sin is. While our conscience often recognizes
obvious wrongdoing, there are sins that become clear only when God’s
Word speaks directly to them. The Scriptures reveal attitudes and
desires that may otherwise remain hidden. In doing so, they lovingly
call us to repentance and greater obedience.

The law also uncovers the ongoing struggle within every believer. Even
after coming to Christ, there remains a conflict between the desires
of the flesh and the work of God’s Spirit. Paul speaks honestly about
this battle, reminding us that spiritual maturity is not found in
pretending the struggle does not exist, but in continually turning to
Christ for strength and mercy.

Most importantly, the law points us to our Savior. Left to ourselves,
we cannot overcome sin or earn God’s favor. The law reveals our
inability, but the gospel proclaims God’s provision. Jesus bore the
penalty for our sin and gives us His Spirit to help us walk in
obedience. What the law could never accomplish through human effort,
Christ accomplishes through His grace.

As you read God’s Word today, do not approach it merely as a list of
commands. Receive it as a gracious gift that reveals God’s holiness,
exposes your need, and continually directs your heart to Jesus Christ.
