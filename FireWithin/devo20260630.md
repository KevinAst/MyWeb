Today's Scripture reminds us that a life shaped by God's presence
leaves a lasting testimony.

As David neared the end of his life, he reflected on the work God had
done through him. David had been many things, shepherd, warrior, king,
and leader of a nation. Yet among the descriptions recorded about him
is one that reveals the deepest priority of his heart: he was known as
the sweet psalmist of Israel. More than military victories or royal
accomplishments, David desired to be remembered as a man who enjoyed
fellowship with the Lord.

David's life was not without failure. He openly acknowledged that he
had not always lived as faithfully as he should have. His family
experienced pain, and his own sins brought serious
consequences. Nevertheless, David rested in God's covenant mercy. He
understood that his standing before God was not based upon flawless
performance, but upon the faithfulness and grace of the Lord.

This remains true for believers today. The accomplishments of this
world, though not unimportant, are temporary. Positions, achievements,
and recognition eventually fade. What endures is a life cultivated in
communion with God through His Word, prayer, worship, and
obedience. The Lord desires that His people walk closely with Him, not
merely serve Him from a distance.

As we abide in Christ, our lives increasingly reflect His
character. Others may remember our work or achievements, but the
greatest testimony we can leave is that we loved the Lord and walked
faithfully with Him.
