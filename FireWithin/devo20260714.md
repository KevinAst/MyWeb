Today’s Scripture reminds us that everything worthy in our lives
ultimately points back to the Lord.

As Solomon built the temple, every measurement, material, and
furnishing reflected God’s careful design and holy purpose. The temple
was not built to display the greatness of a king but to declare the
greatness of God. Even its beauty reminded the people that the Lord is
worthy of reverence, worship, and obedience. Scripture also warns us
through the example of Herod that seeking glory for ourselves leads
only to pride and judgment. God alone deserves the honor that belongs
to Him.

The Lord’s attention to every detail of the temple also reveals
something precious about His character. He is not distant or
unconcerned. The God who carefully prescribed every aspect of His
house is the same God who knows every detail of our lives. Nothing
escapes His notice. He sees our joys, our burdens, our victories, and
our struggles. His care is personal because His love is steadfast.

As followers of Christ, we no longer come to God through an earthly
temple or a system of sacrifices. Jesus Christ is our perfect High
Priest, and through His sacrifice we have direct access to the
Father. This privilege should deepen our humility. Whatever gifts,
opportunities, or accomplishments we possess are expressions of God’s
grace, not reasons for self-exaltation.

Each day presents quiet opportunities to honor the Lord. We glorify
Him by serving faithfully, speaking truthfully, loving others
sincerely, and remembering that every good thing comes from His
hand. A heart that delights in giving God the glory finds lasting joy
because it rests in the One who never changes.

May we live in such a way that our lives direct attention to the
greatness of God rather than to ourselves.
