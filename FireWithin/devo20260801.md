Today’s Scripture reminds us that Jesus is not distant from the
struggles of His people.

The book of Hebrews presents Jesus as the perfect and eternal High
Priest. Under the old covenant, the high priest represented the people
before God by offering sacrifices for sin. Yet every earthly priest
was himself a sinner and needed atonement. His ministry pointed
forward to something greater that God had planned from the beginning.

Jesus fulfilled what every priest could only foreshadow. Because He
lived a sinless life, His sacrifice was complete and sufficient for
all who trust in Him. He does not merely understand our weaknesses
from a distance. Having taken on human flesh, He experienced
temptation, sorrow, weariness, and suffering, yet He remained without
sin. This means that when we come to Him in prayer, we come before One
who knows our struggles perfectly and intercedes for us with
compassion.

This truth also gives the believer confidence. We do not need an
earthly mediator to bring us before God. Through Christ alone we have
access to the throne of grace, where mercy and help are always
available in our time of need. Our confidence is not rooted in our own
faithfulness, but in the finished work of our Savior, who forever
serves as our High Priest before the Father.

Hebrews also reminds believers to grow beyond spiritual infancy. God
desires that His people mature through the steady study and
application of His Word so they can discern truth, walk in obedience,
and know Him more deeply. Spiritual maturity comes through continual
dependence on Christ and faithful attention to Scripture.

Rest today in the assurance that your Savior not only understands your
need, but also continually represents you before the Father.
