Today’s Scripture reminds us that God has established authority for
His purposes, even when earthly leaders are imperfect.

God is a God of order, not confusion. Throughout Scripture He has
established institutions to bring stability and blessing to human
society. Civil government is one of those institutions. While
governments are made up of imperfect people, their authority
ultimately exists because God has allowed it. This truth calls
believers to live as respectful and responsible citizens, honoring
those in authority and obeying laws that do not conflict with God’s
commands.

The apostle Paul wrote these truths while living under a government
that was far from righteous. Yet he reminded believers that God
remains sovereign over every ruler and every nation. Our confidence is
never placed in human leaders but in the Lord who raises up kingdoms
and removes them according to His perfect wisdom. Even when we do not
understand His purposes, we can trust that He is never absent from the
affairs of this world.

At the same time, Scripture also teaches that our highest allegiance
belongs to God alone. When earthly authority commands what God forbids
or forbids what God commands, believers must obey the Lord above all
else. Such obedience may come with hardship, but faithfulness to
Christ has always required courage. The goal is never rebellion for
its own sake, but humble obedience to the One whose authority is
absolute.

As followers of Christ, we are called to demonstrate both respect for
authority and unwavering devotion to God’s Word. In doing so, we bear
witness that our hope rests not in governments, but in the eternal
King whose kingdom will never pass away.

May your heart remain steady today, honoring rightful authority while
keeping Christ firmly on the throne of your life.
