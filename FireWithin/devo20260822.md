Today’s Scripture calls us to recognize that God alone deserves the
worship and trust of our hearts.

Throughout M{ bibleLink(`isa.44@@Isaiah 44 through 46`) }M, the Lord
repeatedly contrasts Himself with the idols Israel had chosen to
worship. Those idols were fashioned from wood and metal by human
hands. They could not speak, answer prayer, rescue, or save. Yet the
people had turned from the living God to things they themselves had
created.

Their problem was more than the presence of carved images. Their
hearts had transferred to created things the worship that belonged to
the Creator. That remains the danger of idolatry. Whatever takes God’s
rightful place in our affection, dependence, or obedience becomes a
rival for our worship. We may never bow before an image, yet we can
place our confidence in ourselves, possessions, accomplishments,
relationships, or human wisdom.

God calls His people away from such empty substitutes by reminding
them who He is. He is the Creator who made all things. He knows and
declares what is to come. He is righteous, sovereign, and unlike
anything human hands or imagination could produce. Most importantly,
He is the God who saves.

That truth directs our attention to Jesus Christ. Sin separates us
from God, and nothing we possess, accomplish, or create can reconcile
us to Him. Salvation must come from God Himself. In His grace, He sent
His Son to bear our sin and provide the forgiveness we could never
earn. Christ is not one spiritual option among many. He is God’s
provision for sinners who turn to Him in faith.

Examine where your confidence rests today. Everything created will
eventually fail us, but the Lord remains worthy of complete trust,
worship, and obedience.
