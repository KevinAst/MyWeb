### The Singing Bible

Experience God's Word in a unique and memorable way — **the entire
Bible set to song!**

Site: <a title="Launch this site in a new window" target="_blank" href="https://thesinging.bible/">The Singing Bible</a>

> Using this site:
> - Choose a Bible book and chapter, pick a style and voice
> - Press `Play`
>   - and enjoy the music of "The Singing Bible"
>   - each time you load the site/app, it picks right up where you left off!

I had no idea that [Bible Hub](https://biblehub.com/) had put the
_entire Bible_ to music!  This site is surprisingly enjoyable and
exceptionally well done. Every book, every chapter, word-for-word, set
to song!

There are 22 different music styles in both male and female voices.
The arrangements are generally modern and engaging. It's a wonderful
way to experience God's Word! Meditate on Scripture and hide it in
your heart—wherever your day takes you.

- This is obviously AI generated, and some people may not like it
  because of that.  While I am careful to never use AI for anything
  that is opinionated, in this case the lyric is quoting Scripture
  word-for-word.

- Others may think it is cheesy.  I personally think that if you match
  the appropriate style to the specific scripture passage, it can be
  worshipful.  The lyric is always clear and intelligible.

`Please Note:` In addition to the web interface, there is also a
<mark>Free Mobile App</mark> for both iPhone and Android. This app
provides the following additional features:
- seamless operation for continuous playback on sleeping phones
- download chapters for offline listening and avoid using mobile data

The Singing Bible uses the 
[Berean Standard Bible (BSB)](https://berean.bible/), 
a translation designed to communicate the meaning of the original
Greek and Hebrew in clear, natural English, with an emphasis on
reading, study, memorization, and evangelism.
