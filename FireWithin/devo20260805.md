Today’s Scripture reminds us that God welcomes those who come to Him
with humble and repentant hearts.

When Solomon dedicated the temple, he understood that the beauty of
the building was not what mattered most. The Lord cannot be contained
by walls or limited to one place. Yet Solomon knew that God delights
to hear the prayers of those who sincerely seek Him. Throughout his
prayer, he repeatedly asked the Lord to hear, forgive, and restore His
people whenever they turned back to Him after their sin.

This reveals something wonderful about the character of God. He is
holy and just, but He is also merciful. Sin brings painful
consequences, often adding unnecessary hardship to the trials of life
in a fallen world. Even so, the Lord does not delight in leaving His
people under the weight of their failures. His desire is that they
would recognize their need, confess their sin, and return to Him with
genuine repentance.

Through Jesus Christ, we have the assurance that forgiveness is
available to everyone who comes before God in faith. Our confidence
does not rest in our own goodness, but in the finished work of Christ
on the cross. Because of His sacrifice, we can approach the Father
knowing that He is faithful to forgive and restore those who humble
themselves before Him.

If the Holy Spirit brings conviction today, do not resist His
voice. Bring your heart honestly before the Lord. Confession is not
merely admitting wrong, it is agreeing with God and turning back to
Him in trust. His mercy is never exhausted toward those who truly
repent.

May your heart rest in this truth today, our faithful God is always
ready to receive those who come to Him in humility.
