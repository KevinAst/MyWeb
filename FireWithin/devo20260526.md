The Bible repeatedly reminds us that God is attentive to His covenant
purposes and faithful to His promises. Throughout Scripture, Israel
stands as a testimony to both the justice and mercy of God. Nations
rise and fall, conflicts come and go, yet the Lord remains sovereign
over history. He neither abandons His people nor loses sight of His
redemptive plan.

M{ bibleLink(`psa.121@@Psalms 121`) }M was written as a song of trust
for pilgrims traveling toward Jerusalem. The psalmist understood that
human strength is limited and earthly security can quickly fail. Yet
he found peace in knowing that the Keeper of Israel never grows
weary. God’s watchful care is constant, perfect, and unchanging.

When we see turmoil in the world, especially surrounding Israel, we
should not respond with fear or speculation. Instead, believers are
called to pray, to remain grounded in Scripture, and to remember that
Christ Himself is the center of God’s redemptive plan. Jesus was born
in Israel, crucified in Jerusalem, raised from the dead, and promised
to return again. The Lord’s purposes through Christ will stand,
regardless of the unrest of nations.

At the same time, Scripture teaches us to value every human life. We
should pray for peace, for the protection of innocent people, and for
hearts to turn toward the Savior. The gospel remains the only lasting
hope for Jew and Gentile alike. Sin divides, hatred destroys, but
Christ alone reconciles sinners to God and offers true peace.

As followers of Jesus, we must guard our hearts from bitterness, fear,
and confusion by staying anchored in the truth of God’s Word.
