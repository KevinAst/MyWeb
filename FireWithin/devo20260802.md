Today’s Scripture reminds us that true hope is not something we
create, but something God gives.

The world often places its confidence in circumstances, personal
ability, or the promise that tomorrow will somehow be better. Yet
those foundations cannot bear the weight of life’s greatest
trials. Scripture directs our attention to a different source. Paul
calls the Lord the God of hope because hope finds its origin in Him,
not in ourselves. When our faith rests on Christ, our confidence is
anchored in the One who never changes.

Romans 15 also reminds us that God is the God of patience, comfort,
hope, and peace. These are not merely qualities He possesses. They are
gifts He graciously supplies to His children according to their
need. He patiently bears with us as we grow in faith. He comforts us
in seasons of sorrow. He fills us with hope when discouragement
threatens. He grants peace that remains steady even when circumstances
are uncertain.

Our hope in Christ is not wishful thinking or positive optimism. It is
a settled confidence that the Lord is faithful to every promise He has
made. Because Jesus has conquered sin and death through His cross and
resurrection, believers can stand securely even when everything around
them seems unstable. Our security rests in the character of God, not
in the changing conditions of life.

Today, consider where your confidence is placed. If your heart has
become troubled by uncertainty, return to the Lord in prayer and
remember that He alone is the solid foundation for every season of
life.

Let your heart rest in this truth: the strength of your hope depends
not on the firmness of your grip on Christ, but on the faithfulness of
His grip on you.
