Today's Scripture reminds us that our identity in Christ shapes the
way we live.

Romans 6 teaches that the Christian life is not merely about receiving
forgiveness, it is also about living in the reality of what Christ has
accomplished for us. Through faith in Jesus, believers are united with
Him in His death and resurrection. Paul explains that our "old self"
was crucified with Christ so that sin would no longer rule over
us. This does not mean that temptation disappears, but it does mean
that sin is no longer our master.

Many believers become discouraged because they still struggle with
sin. Scripture acknowledges this battle, yet it calls us to remember
what is true. God declares that we are new creations in Christ. The
old life, marked by self-rule and rebellion, no longer defines us. We
are now called to walk in newness of life, depending daily upon the
Holy Spirit for strength and obedience.

Victory over sin begins by believing God's Word rather than trusting
our changing feelings. We must continually reckon, or count, ourselves
dead to sin and alive to God. This is an intentional act of
faith. Each day we choose whether we will yield ourselves to the
desires of the flesh or present ourselves to God as instruments of
righteousness.

The Lord does not lead His children merely by rules. He leads us
through a relationship of grace and love. Because Christ has loved us
and given Himself for us, we are motivated to pursue holiness, not to
earn God's favor, but because we already belong to Him.
