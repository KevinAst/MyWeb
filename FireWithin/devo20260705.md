Today’s Scripture reminds us that the Christian life is not lived by
human determination alone.

Every believer knows the struggle between the desire to honor God and
the pull of the sinful nature. Although we have been made new in
Christ, we still live in bodies that are weak and prone to
temptation. Scripture does not ignore this conflict. Instead, it
points us to the One who enables us to persevere.

Romans 8 reminds us that the Holy Spirit dwells within every
believer. The same Spirit who raised Jesus from the dead is present to
strengthen us for daily obedience. This does not mean the battle
disappears, but it does mean we are not left to fight it alone. God
has provided His own Spirit to empower us to turn from sin and walk in
righteousness.

The Holy Spirit also assures us that we belong to the Father. Through
Christ we have been adopted into God’s family, not as distant servants
but as beloved children. Our obedience is not an attempt to earn His
acceptance. It is the grateful response of those who have already
received His grace.

There are also moments when life’s burdens leave us unable to express
what is in our hearts. Even then, we are not abandoned. The Holy
Spirit intercedes for us according to the will of God. When our words
fail, God’s Spirit faithfully ministers on our behalf.

As you walk through today, remember that victory over sin and
endurance in hardship are rooted in God’s presence, not merely your
own resolve. Christ has given His Spirit so that you may live
faithfully, even in the midst of the ongoing battle.

Rest today in the assurance that the God who dwells within you is
also the God who faithfully sustains you.
