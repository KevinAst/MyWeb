This passage reveals that God provides a sure foundation even in the
midst of warning and judgment.

M{ bibleLink(`isa.28@@Isaiah 28`) }M describes people who had become
confident in false securities. Both Israel and Judah had wandered from
God, and their confidence rested in arrangements, assumptions, and
human strength that could not protect them. Yet in the middle of this
sober warning, God gives a promise. He declares that He is laying a
tested and precious cornerstone in Zion.

The New Testament identifies this cornerstone as Jesus Christ. He is
not merely one part of the believer’s life. He is the foundation upon
which the whole life is meant to rest. A cornerstone determined the
alignment and stability of a building. In the same way, every part of
our lives must be brought into alignment with Christ.

Jesus is a tested stone. He remained faithful through temptation,
rejection, suffering, and the cross. He is precious because there is
no one like Him. He is also a sure foundation because those who trust
in Him are resting upon One who cannot fail.

It is possible to confess Christ as Savior while still keeping areas
of life largely untouched by His lordship. Our relationships, work,
finances, words, thoughts, and private choices are all meant to be
shaped by Him. Scripture does not call us to fit Jesus conveniently
into the structure we have already built. It calls us to build upon
Him.

God’s grace does not excuse our wandering. It brings us back to the
One who forgives, corrects, and restores. Christ receives sinners with
mercy, yet He also calls us to leave sin and walk in obedience.

Consider whether the structure of your life is truly being aligned
with Christ, or whether something else has quietly become your
foundation.
