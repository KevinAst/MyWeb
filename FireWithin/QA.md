## Questions and Answers

{{book.CornerstoneChapel}} holds annual Q&A services _(Mid-Year and
Year-End)_, where Pastor Gary _(joined by sons Tyler and Austin)_
answers Bible questions submitted by the congregation!

The questions cataloged on this page begin in 2021 and continue forward.

> **<mark>NOTE:</mark>** Unlike most FireWithin links, this page uses YouTube
> references rather than Cornerstone links. This provides the ability to
> jump directly to individual questions within each Q&A session.
> 
> - Each question is individually itemized, making it easy to search for
>   topics or words of interest _(in your browser)_.
> 
> - For each Q&A session, you can either watch the entire session from
>   beginning to end, or jump directly to the question that interests you.
