Today’s Scripture reminds us that God’s faithfulness is never limited
by what appears impossible.

The vision of the valley of dry bones was given to Ezekiel during one
of Israel’s darkest seasons. The nation had been scattered, Jerusalem
had fallen, and hope seemed gone. Humanly speaking, there was no path
back. Yet God asked a question that directed attention away from
circumstances and toward His power: "Can these bones live?" The answer
rested not in human ability but in the Lord who speaks life where
death has prevailed.

God promised to gather His people, restore them to their land, and
breathe life into what appeared beyond recovery. His purpose was not
merely to bless Israel but to display His own faithfulness before the
nations. The Lord would accomplish exactly what He had declared
because His name and His character are trustworthy.

This same God has not changed. While the historical promises of
Ezekiel belong specifically to Israel, they reveal an unchanging truth
about the Lord. He is faithful to every promise He has made. Through
Jesus Christ, believers have the assurance that God forgives,
restores, and completes the work He begins in those who trust Him. Our
confidence is not rooted in our strength or circumstances but in His
unchanging Word.

There are seasons when life can resemble a valley of dry bones, when
hope feels distant and answers seem delayed. Yet God’s purposes are
never hindered by what appears impossible to us. He calls His people
to trust His Word, remain faithful, and wait upon His perfect timing.

Rest today in the certainty that the God who speaks is also the God
who fulfills what He has spoken.
