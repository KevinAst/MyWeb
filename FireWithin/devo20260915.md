Jeremiah’s prayer comes in a chapter that exposes the foolishness of
idolatry. The nations fashioned gods from wood and precious metals,
then depended upon what their own hands had made. In contrast,
Jeremiah declares that the Lord alone is the true and living God.

Idolatry, however, is not limited to carved images. The deeper problem
is the human heart giving to something created the devotion that
belongs to the Creator. A possession, relationship, career, pleasure,
ambition, or even something good can become an idol when it begins to
compete with our affection for God.

Jeremiah responds to this danger with humility: his life is not his
own, and he cannot safely direct his own steps apart from God. That
same posture belongs in the life of every believer. We belong to the
Lord because Christ purchased us at the cost of His own
blood. Therefore, our time, bodies, resources, desires, and plans are
all to be surrendered to His lordship.

This also means we need God’s correction. Jeremiah did not merely
recognize the idolatry around him. He asked God to correct him. That
is the posture of a heart willing to be searched and changed by the
Lord.

God’s discipline is not evidence that He has abandoned His
children. His loving correction exposes what has begun to rule us so
that we might repent and return to wholehearted devotion. Rather than
resisting His correction, we should receive it with humility and ask
Him to direct our steps according to His Word.

Consider quietly today whether anything has been receiving the
affection, obedience, time, or trust that rightly belongs to God
alone.
