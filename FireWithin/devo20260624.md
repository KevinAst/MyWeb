Today's Scripture reminds us that the Christian life is not merely
about beginning well, but about finishing faithfully.

As Paul addressed the elders from Ephesus, he reflected on a life
marked by humility, suffering, and steadfast devotion to
Christ. Though he knew hardships awaited him, Paul remained committed
to the calling God had given him. His greatest desire was not personal
comfort or safety, but to complete the race set before him with
joy. This perspective shaped every decision he made.

The Christian life is often compared in Scripture to a race, not
because it is competitive, but because it requires endurance. Every
believer begins this race when he or she places faith in Jesus
Christ. Along the way there will be seasons of joy and seasons of
tears. Trials, disappointments, and spiritual battles are not signs
that God has abandoned us. Rather, they are reminders that we must
continually depend upon Him. Paul endured many hardships, yet he
pressed forward because his eyes were fixed on Christ rather than his
circumstances.

Humility is essential if we are to finish well. Pride turns our hearts
toward self-reliance, while humility keeps us dependent upon God's
grace. The Lord does not ask us to run in our own strength. He calls
us to walk faithfully, trusting that He will sustain us each step of
the way. As we surrender our ambitions, fears, and desires to Him, we
discover that His grace is sufficient for the journey.

The race set before you may be difficult, but Christ, who called you,
is also the One who will faithfully carry you to the finish line.
