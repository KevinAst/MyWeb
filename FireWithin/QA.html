Questions and Answers

Cornerstone Chapel holds annual Q&A services (Mid-Year and Year-End), where Pastor Gary (joined by sons Tyler and Austin) answers Bible questions submitted by the congregation!

The questions cataloged on this page begin in 2021 and continue forward.

NOTE: Unlike most FireWithin links, this page uses YouTube references rather than Cornerstone links. This provides the ability to jump directly to individual questions within each Q&A session.

  • Each question is individually itemized, making it easy to search for topics or words of interest (in your browser).

  • For each Q&A session, you can either watch the entire session from beginning to end, or jump directly to the question that interests you.
