Today’s Scripture reminds us that our identity in Christ should shape
the way we live each day.

When we trusted in Jesus Christ for salvation, God did more than
forgive our sins. He gave us new life. The believer is no longer
defined by the old sinful nature but by a new identity that is hidden
with Christ. Because of this, Paul calls us to seek the things above
and to set our minds on heavenly realities. This does not mean
withdrawing from everyday responsibilities. Rather, it means allowing
the hope of Christ and the promise of eternity to guide our thoughts,
priorities, and decisions.

The Christian life involves a daily choice. The old patterns of sin
still seek to draw us back, but they no longer have authority over
us. Through the power of the Holy Spirit, we are called to put away
attitudes and behaviors that belong to our former way of life and to
put on compassion, kindness, humility, patience, and love. These
qualities reflect the character of Christ and demonstrate that He is
at work within us.

One of the clearest expressions of this new life is forgiveness. Just
as Christ has completely forgiven us, we are called to forgive
others. Forgiveness is not a denial of wrong, but a willing release of
the offense into God’s hands. It frees our hearts from bitterness and
allows us to walk in the grace we ourselves have received.

As you go through today, remember that your true life is found in
Christ. Let your heart remain fixed on Him, even while your hands
faithfully serve Him in the ordinary responsibilities of life.
