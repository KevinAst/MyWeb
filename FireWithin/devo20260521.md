The Bible teaches that even during the Tribulation, God will continue
extending the opportunity for people to repent and believe in Jesus
Christ. His judgment upon the earth will be severe, but His mercy will
still be evident. Scripture consistently reveals that God is patient
and desires people to turn to Him before it is too late.

In Revelation, we read about 144,000 Jews whom God seals and uses as
witnesses throughout the earth. These believers will proclaim the
truth during a time of widespread deception and rebellion. In
addition, M{ bibleLink(`rev.14@@Revelation 14`) }M describes an angel
flying through the midst of heaven proclaiming the everlasting gospel
to the nations. This is a remarkable demonstration of God’s
grace. Even in a season of great tribulation, the Lord continues
calling people to salvation.

At the same time, the Bible makes clear that following Christ during
that period will come at a tremendous cost. Many who trust in Christ
will face persecution and death because they refuse to worship the
Antichrist or receive the mark of the beast. Their faith will be
genuine, and their allegiance to Christ will be tested severely. Yet
the Lord will sustain His people, and their eternal hope will remain
secure.

This truth should not lead anyone to delay repentance. Scripture
repeatedly urges people to respond to God today. None of us are
guaranteed tomorrow, and the human heart can grow harder through
continued resistance to the truth. God’s grace is available now
through Jesus Christ, who died for our sins and rose again so that all
who trust in Him might be saved.
