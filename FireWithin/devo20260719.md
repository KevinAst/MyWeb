Today’s Scripture reminds us that our greatest hope is found in the
person of Jesus Christ.

When Jesus arrived in Bethany, Lazarus had already been in the tomb
for four days. To Mary and Martha, it appeared that Jesus had come too
late. Yet the Lord was not absent from their suffering, nor was He
unaware of their grief. His delay was not a lack of love but part of
His perfect purpose. Even as He prepared to display His power over
death, Jesus entered into the sorrow of those He loved. His tears
remind us that the Son of God is both sovereign and compassionate.

Jesus did not simply promise a future resurrection. He declared that
He Himself is the resurrection and the life. Eternal life is not
merely a gift apart from Christ. It is found in Him. Through His death
and resurrection, Jesus conquered sin and death so that all who trust
in Him may have the assurance of everlasting life.

This truth also shapes how believers face the trials of this present
life. We may not understand God’s timing, and His ways often differ
from our expectations. Yet His purposes are always wise and good. The
same Lord who called Lazarus from the tomb remains faithful today. He
is worthy of our trust even when His plans unfold differently than we
had hoped.

Because Christ lives, those who belong to Him can face both life and
death with confidence, knowing that His promises cannot fail.

Rest today in the certainty that your hope is anchored not in changing
circumstances but in the unchanging Savior who has overcome the grave.
