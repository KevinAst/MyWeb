Today’s Scripture reminds us that unity in the body of Christ is
strengthened when believers choose love over personal preference.

Romans 14 addresses an issue that has challenged God’s people
throughout history. Christians often come from different backgrounds,
experiences, and convictions. In the church at Rome, Jewish and
Gentile believers disagreed over matters such as food and the
observance of certain days. These were not questions of the gospel
itself, but matters of personal conscience. Paul did not call them to
abandon biblical truth. Instead, he urged them to stop judging one
another over issues where Scripture had not given a direct command.

This passage teaches us that spiritual maturity is not measured by
winning arguments or proving ourselves right. It is seen in our
willingness to love fellow believers, respect sincere convictions, and
avoid using our freedom in ways that cause another Christian to
stumble. God has received those who belong to Christ, and we should be
careful not to reject those whom He has accepted.

The Lord often places people with different personalities, gifts, and
perspectives together so that His church will be strengthened. What
may seem like an inconvenience can become a blessing when humility
replaces pride. Instead of allowing differences to create division, we
are called to pursue peace and seek the spiritual good of one another.

Christ Himself is our perfect example. He did not seek His own comfort
but gave Himself in love for those He came to save. As we fix our eyes
on Him, we learn to value unity without compromising truth and to
extend grace where Scripture allows freedom.

May your heart today be more concerned with building up God’s people
than with defending your own preferences.
