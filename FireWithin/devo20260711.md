Today’s Scripture reminds us that knowing God’s will begins with a
heart that is surrendered to Him.

Many believers desire to know God’s direction for important decisions,
yet Scripture teaches that discerning His will is closely connected to
living in obedience. Paul urges us not merely to seek guidance, but to
present ourselves as living sacrifices to God. A life set apart for
Him becomes a life that is increasingly able to recognize His leading.

The transformation Paul describes begins with the renewing of the
mind. Instead of allowing our thinking to be shaped by the values and
priorities of the world, God calls us to think according to His
Word. As our minds are renewed through Scripture, prayer, and
fellowship with Him, our ability to recognize His wisdom grows
stronger. His will becomes clearer, not because every question is
immediately answered, but because our hearts are learning to desire
what pleases Him.

There will be times when we do not fully understand God’s timing or
His purposes. Even then, we can trust His character. He is a gracious
Father who patiently leads His children, correcting, teaching, and
directing them as they walk with Him. Our confidence rests not in our
ability to discern perfectly, but in His faithfulness to guide those
who sincerely seek Him.

When facing decisions, let God’s Word shape your thinking before the
world shapes your expectations. A surrendered life and a renewed mind
provide fertile ground for faithful discernment, allowing you to walk
with confidence in the Lord rather than in your own understanding.

The safest place is not where life seems easiest, but where Christ
is leading.
