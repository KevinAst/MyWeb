The Bible presents a very different view of humanity than the one
often embraced by the world. While many believe that people are
basically good and simply need improvement, Scripture teaches that the
problem runs much deeper. Sin has affected every human heart, and
because of that, no one stands righteous before a holy God. This truth
may be difficult to hear, but it is necessary because we cannot
appreciate God's remedy until we understand our condition.

God has not left humanity without witness. His power and existence are
evident in creation, and His Word reveals His character and His
standards. Yet when people continually reject Him, they begin to
exchange truth for falsehood and pursue their own desires apart from
His design. The result is confusion, brokenness, and a life
increasingly disconnected from the wisdom and goodness of God.

The encouraging truth is that God does not expose our sin in order to
condemn us without hope. He reveals the reality of our condition so
that we will recognize our need for a Savior. The same God who
identifies the disease of sin has provided the cure through Jesus
Christ. At the cross, God's justice and mercy met perfectly. There,
Jesus bore the penalty we deserved so that all who trust in Him might
receive forgiveness, reconciliation, and new life.

As believers, we should approach this truth with humility. We are not
saved because we were better than others. We are saved by grace alone
through faith in Christ alone. This keeps us dependent on God and
compassionate toward those who have not yet come to know Him. Our
calling is to hold firmly to God's truth while extending His love to a
world that desperately needs both.
