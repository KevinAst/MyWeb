Today's Scripture reminds us that the Christian life is sustained not
by our strength, but by God's grace.

As the apostle Paul prepared to part from the Ephesian elders, he
entrusted them to God and to "the word of His grace." Paul knew that
he would no longer be present to encourage, teach, or shepherd
them. Yet he was confident that God's grace would continue the work in
their lives. This passage reveals an important truth for every
believer. Our security rests not in human support, but in the faithful
care of God.

Grace is God's unmerited favor shown to us through Jesus Christ. It is
not something we earn through good works or moral effort. Scripture
certainly calls us to holiness, yet God's law was never intended to
save us. Rather, it reveals God's holy character and exposes our need
for a Savior. Left to ourselves, we fall short of God's perfect
standard.

This is why the gospel is such good news. Jesus came into the world to
accomplish what we never could. Through His death and resurrection, He
offers forgiveness for our sins and His righteousness to all who trust
in Him by faith. Grace reminds us that our standing before God rests
on Christ's finished work, not our imperfect performance.

Paul says that God's grace builds us up and gives us an inheritance
among those who are sanctified. Grace strengthens weary hearts, lifts
the burden of guilt, and assures us that eternal life is secure in
Christ. Because Jesus died and rose again, believers have an
inheritance that can never perish, spoil, or fade.

Entrust both your life and those you love to God's faithful hands,
knowing that His grace is sufficient for every need.
