Today’s Scripture reminds us that God’s encouragement often comes when
we need it most.

Paul had faithfully preached Christ, yet he found himself in prison
because of his obedience. Instead of removing the trial, Jesus came to
reassure him. His words, "Be of good cheer," were not based on easier
circumstances, but on God’s unchanging purpose.

The Lord reminded Paul that his ministry was not over. The same God
who had called him to testify in Jerusalem would also lead him to
Rome. Even imprisonment would become part of God’s plan to bring the
gospel before those who needed to hear it.

We also face seasons of uncertainty, disappointment, and
hardship. While we may not understand God’s ways, we can trust His
character. He does not waste our trials. He uses them to deepen our
faith and accomplish His purposes in ways we cannot always see.

Faithfulness is what God asks of us. The results belong to Him. As we
continue to walk with Christ, He supplies the strength and courage we
need for each step.

Remember today that the Lord who calls you is the Lord who stands
beside you.
