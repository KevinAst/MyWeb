Today’s Scripture reminds us that true strength is found in faithful
obedience to God.

As David’s life drew to a close, he knew his greatest gift to Solomon
would not be political advice or military strategy. Instead, he
pointed his son to the Lord. David understood that the
responsibilities awaiting Solomon would require more than natural
ability. They would require a heart that remained anchored in God’s
Word.

David’s instruction reveals an enduring truth for every believer. The
Lord never calls His people to fulfill His purposes apart from His
presence. Strength and courage are not produced by self-confidence but
by walking closely with God. Obedience is not merely a duty. It is the
pathway through which we experience His wisdom, guidance, and
sustaining grace.

David’s own life gave weight to these words. He knew both the
blessings of trusting God and the painful consequences of
sin. Although he had failed deeply, he had also experienced the mercy
of God through sincere repentance. His final counsel reflected a life
that had learned the faithfulness of the Lord through both triumph and
failure.

Every season of life brings responsibilities that can feel beyond our
strength. Whether leading a family, serving in ministry, or quietly
honoring Christ in daily work, the call remains the same. Walk in
God’s ways. Hold fast to His Word. Trust Him to provide what He
commands.

Our greatest example is Jesus Christ, who perfectly obeyed the Father
in every circumstance. Through Him we receive both forgiveness for our
failures and grace to live faithfully each day.

As you walk through today, remember that lasting strength does not
come from your own abilities, but from remaining faithful to the God
who is always faithful.
