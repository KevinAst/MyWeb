Today’s Scripture reminds us that God often accomplishes His purposes
through paths we would never choose for ourselves.

When Paul remained imprisoned for years, it may have appeared that his
ministry had been interrupted. Yet the Lord had already assured him
that he would one day testify in Rome. Between that promise and its
fulfillment were delays, false accusations, trials, and hardship. None
of these events were outside God’s sovereign care. Each one became an
opportunity for Paul to bear witness to Christ before rulers and
influential leaders who otherwise may never have heard the gospel.

God’s plans are often different from our expectations. We naturally
desire the shortest path to the destination, but the Lord is concerned
with far more than our arrival. He is accomplishing His purposes in us
and through us along the way. The unexpected interruptions, difficult
seasons, and changes in direction may become the very places where His
grace is displayed and His truth is proclaimed.

Like Paul, every believer has a testimony of God’s saving work. Some
testimonies are dramatic, while others reflect a lifetime of God’s
preserving grace. Neither is greater than the other. The power is not
found in the details of our story but in the Savior who redeemed
us. God calls His people to be faithful, leaving the results in His
hands. Our responsibility is to obey, to speak of Christ when He opens
the door, and to trust Him with the outcome.

As you walk through today, ask the Lord to help you recognize the
people He places in your path. What may seem like an interruption
could be one of His appointed opportunities to display His grace
through your life.

Remember that God’s delays are never wasted when they are directed by
His sovereign hand.
