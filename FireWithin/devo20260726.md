Today’s Scripture reminds us that the resurrection of Jesus Christ
stands at the center of God’s plan of redemption.

The resurrection is not merely the conclusion of Christ’s earthly
ministry, but the confirmation that His sacrifice for sin was accepted
by the Father. Jesus willingly endured the cross, was buried, and on
the third day rose bodily from the grave. In doing so, He conquered
sin and death and secured eternal hope for all who place their faith
in Him.

The Bible presents the resurrection as a historical reality witnessed
by many. Because Christ is alive, our faith rests on a living Savior
rather than on religious tradition or human effort. Christianity is
founded upon a relationship with the risen Lord, who calls sinners to
repentance and offers forgiveness through His grace.

The resurrection also reveals the heart of God. Though humanity is
broken by sin, God does not abandon those He created. Instead, He
pursues us through His Son, providing the only way to be reconciled to
Himself. This is not earned by good works but received by faith in
Jesus Christ.

As believers, we are called to live in light of the empty tomb. Our
confidence is not rooted in changing circumstances but in the
unchanging truth that Christ reigns. His victory assures us that sin
has been defeated, death has lost its final sting, and His promises
remain certain. Every day is an opportunity to walk in gratitude,
obedience, and hope because our Savior lives.

May the reality of the risen Christ shape both your faith and your
daily walk, reminding you that the One who overcame the grave is
faithful to sustain all who belong to Him.
