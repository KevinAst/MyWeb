Today’s Scripture reminds us that our relationship with Christ is
revealed in the way we treat other people.

Paul begins the practical section of Romans by turning the believer’s
attention from doctrine to daily living. After explaining God’s saving
grace through Jesus Christ, he calls Christians to demonstrate that
grace in their relationships. Kindness, honor, patience, love, and
humility are not merely admirable qualities. They are evidence of a
heart that has been transformed by the gospel.

Because Christ has loved us when we were undeserving, we are called to
extend that same gracious love to others. This love is not based on
convenience or emotion. It is a deliberate choice that reflects the
character of our Savior. Whether we are showing honor through simple
acts of service, being considerate in our words, accepting fellow
believers with grace, or gently correcting someone for their good,
every action becomes an opportunity to display Christ.

These commands are not limited to moments when relationships are
easy. They become especially important when disagreements arise or
when others fail to respond with the same kindness. Paul reminds us
that, as much as it depends on us, we are to pursue peace. While we
cannot control another person’s response, we are responsible for
walking in obedience to the Lord.

As we grow in Christ, our concern should become less about defending
ourselves and more about reflecting Him. The Holy Spirit enables
believers to live in a way that strengthens the body of Christ and
points others to the grace we have received.
