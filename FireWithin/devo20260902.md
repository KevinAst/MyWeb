Today’s Scripture reminds us that spiritual conflict is real, but the
final victory belongs to God.

Paul closes Romans with a striking promise. He does not tell believers
that they must defeat Satan by their own strength. Instead, he points
them to “the God of peace.” The One who gives peace is also the One
who has authority over the enemy. Satan may oppose, tempt, deceive,
and seek to divide, but he is neither equal with God nor beyond God’s
control.

Scripture teaches believers to be alert without becoming fearful. Our
struggle is not merely against flesh and blood, yet we must also be
careful not to blame every difficulty on spiritual warfare. Sometimes
conflict comes from our own sinful desires, careless words, or poor
choices. Biblical discernment requires humility. We should take
responsibility for our own sin while remaining watchful against the
schemes of the devil.

When spiritual opposition comes, the Christian is not called to panic
or act as though victory depends upon personal power. We are told to
stand firm in the Lord. His truth exposes lies. His righteousness
guards the heart. Faith answers doubt. The assurance of salvation
steadies the mind. The Word of God gives us truth with which to resist
temptation, and prayer keeps us dependent upon the strength of Christ.

Jesus has already triumphed through His death and
resurrection. Therefore, believers resist the enemy from a position of
confidence in Christ, not confidence in themselves. We submit
ourselves to God, remain grounded in Scripture, pray faithfully, and
trust Him with the battle.

When the enemy seeks to unsettle you, remember that your security
rests in the strength and victory of Christ.
