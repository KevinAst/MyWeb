Today’s Scripture reminds us that God faithfully supplies what His
people need to accomplish the work He entrusts to them.

Solomon began his reign as a young king with responsibilities far
beyond his own strength. Although God had already granted him
extraordinary wisdom, Solomon did not attempt to govern Israel
alone. He appointed capable leaders throughout the kingdom,
recognizing that effective leadership requires humility and a
willingness to entrust responsibility to others. His example reminds
us that wisdom is not merely possessing knowledge, but applying God’s
truth with humility and discernment.

The Lord blessed Solomon with wisdom that matched the magnitude of his
calling. As the nation prospered and grew, God provided understanding
sufficient for every challenge before him. This reveals something
important about God’s character. He does not call His people into
service and then leave them to rely on their own abilities. He
graciously provides what is necessary for faithful obedience when we
depend upon Him.

At the same time, the chapter quietly warns against placing confidence
in human strength. Solomon began to accumulate military resources
beyond what God had instructed for Israel’s kings. The Lord desired
His people to remember that their security rested not in horses or
chariots, but in His sovereign care. Even God’s gifts can become
distractions if they lead us to trust ourselves more than Him.

The same principle applies to every believer today. Whether our
responsibilities seem small or overwhelming, Christ calls us to seek
His wisdom daily. He is faithful to supply grace, discernment, and
strength for every assignment He places before us. Our confidence
rests not in our resources, experience, or abilities, but in the God
who equips His servants for His purposes.

Seek God’s wisdom before relying on your own understanding, and
trust that His provision will always be sufficient for the work He has
given you.
