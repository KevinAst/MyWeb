Three kings and their armies had marched seven days through the
wilderness, and now there was no water. Men and animals were
exhausted, and what had begun as a military campaign was quickly
becoming a disaster.

Then the word of the Lord came through Elisha: Dig ditches.

Think about that command. The ground was dry. There were no clouds
gathering overhead, no sound of thunder, and God specifically said
they would see neither wind nor rain. Yet before a single drop
appeared, they were told to prepare for water.

That is faith with work clothes on.

It would have been easy for those men to say, “Lord, send the water
first, and then we’ll start digging.” But God required obedience
before they saw His provision. Their responsibility was not to produce
the miracle. Only God could do that. Their responsibility was to
believe His Word enough to prepare for what He promised.

There is an important distinction here. Biblical faith is not trying
to force God’s hand, nor is it rushing ahead with our own plans and
asking Him to bless them afterward. Faith hears what God has said and
acts accordingly. As James reminds us, “Faith without works is dead”
(M{ bibleLink(`jas.2.26@@James 2:26@@NKJV`) }M).

Perhaps you are waiting upon the Lord today. You have prayed, but
nothing seems to be changing. The landscape is still dry, and there
isn’t a cloud on the horizon. The temptation is to do nothing until
God does something.

But what has He already told you to do?

Has God called you to forgive? Then forgive. Has He shown you an area
of sin that needs to go? Put it away. Has He called you to serve,
give, witness, pray, or take some other step of obedience? Then start
digging.

Notice what happened next: “Suddenly water came by way of Edom, and
the land was filled with water” 
(M{ bibleLink(`2ki.3.20@@2 Kings 3:20@@NKJV`) }M).
The soldiers dug, but God supplied the water. Their obedience did not
create His power; it positioned them to receive what He had promised.

Dear believer, you do not need to understand how God will accomplish
His purposes before you obey Him. Trust His Word and faithfully do
what He has placed before you. Leave the miracle to Him.
