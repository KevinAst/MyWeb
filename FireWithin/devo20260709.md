Today’s Scripture reminds us that God’s invitation to salvation is
extended to every person through Jesus Christ alone.

Paul wrote Romans 9 through 11 with deep sorrow for his fellow Jews
because many had rejected their Messiah. Although Israel had received
God’s covenants, promises, and the privilege of bringing the Messiah
into the world, these blessings did not provide salvation apart from
faith in Christ. Paul’s burden teaches us that religious heritage,
spiritual knowledge, or family background can never replace a personal
relationship with Jesus.

God has always been faithful to His promises. His covenant purposes
for Israel reveal both His sovereignty and His unwavering
character. Yet the same grace that was offered to Israel is also
offered to every nation through the finished work of Christ. At the
foot of the cross, every person stands on equal ground. No one is
accepted because of ancestry, and no one is excluded because of
background. Salvation is received by grace through faith in Jesus
Christ.

This truth calls us to humility. We cannot trust in our
accomplishments, traditions, or outward expressions of faith. Our
confidence rests entirely in Christ, who died for our sins and rose
again. It also calls us to pray for those who have not yet believed,
just as Paul faithfully prayed for his own people. The heart of God
desires that sinners come to repentance and find life in His Son.

May we never take God’s grace for granted, but continually rejoice
that the same Savior who called us also invites all who will believe.
