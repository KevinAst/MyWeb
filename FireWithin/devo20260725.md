Today’s Scripture reminds us that every passing day brings us closer
to the return of Christ.

Paul calls believers to live with spiritual awareness because the
certainty of Christ’s return should shape the way we live today. He
contrasts sleep with wakefulness, darkness with light, and the old
life with the new. These images remind us that following Christ is not
merely about what we believe, but about how we live in response to
what we believe.

Spiritual sleep is more than inactivity. It is allowing our hearts to
grow dull to God’s Word, becoming distracted by temporary concerns, or
settling into patterns that no longer reflect Christ. The Lord calls
His people to remain alert, discerning the times, and faithfully
carrying out the work He has entrusted to them.

Paul also urges believers to cast off the works of darkness and put on
the armor of light. This is not accomplished through human
determination alone, but through daily surrender to Christ. As we grow
in Him, we learn to put aside sinful habits and cultivate the
character that reflects His grace. Kindness, humility, patience,
forgiveness, and purity become the visible evidence of a life
transformed by the gospel.

The battle for holy living often begins in the mind. Before sinful
actions take root, sinful thoughts seek a place to remain. Scripture
calls us to reject those thoughts and instead fill our minds with the
truth of God’s Word. As we abide in Christ, the Holy Spirit
strengthens us to resist temptation and to walk in obedience.

Each day is another opportunity to live with the joyful expectation
that our Savior will return. Until that day, our calling is not to
speculate about the future but to faithfully honor Christ in the
present.

May today’s choices reflect the light of Christ and the hope of His
coming Kingdom.
