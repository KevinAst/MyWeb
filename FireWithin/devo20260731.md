Today’s Scripture reminds us that God calls His people to protect the
unity of His church by remaining faithful to His truth.

As Paul closes his letter to the Romans, he does not end with a
discussion of doctrine alone, but with a loving warning. He knew that
false teaching and selfish ambition could quietly undermine the
fellowship of believers. Division does not begin only with
disagreement. It often begins when pride replaces humility and
personal desires become more important than the well-being of others.

Scripture consistently reveals that God delights in reconciliation
because it reflects His own character. Through Jesus Christ, He has
reconciled sinners to Himself and calls His people to pursue peace
with one another. Satan, however, seeks to divide what God has joined
together. Whether in families, churches, or friendships, division
weakens our witness and distracts us from God’s purposes.

This passage also reminds us that we are not called to control or
change another person’s heart. The Bible instructs us to warn those
who persist in causing division and, when necessary, to distance
ourselves from their harmful influence. The work of changing hearts
belongs to the Lord. Our responsibility is to remain faithful, to
guard our own hearts from pride, and to pursue peace without
compromising biblical truth.

Ask the Lord today to examine your own spirit. Where humility,
patience, and love are present, unity can flourish. As we submit
ourselves to Christ, He enables us to become instruments of His
reconciling grace rather than contributors to unnecessary conflict.

May your words and actions today reflect the peace of Christ, who
unites His people in truth and love.
